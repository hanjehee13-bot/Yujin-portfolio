Case study 03 / Streaming Media UX

Streaming Advertising & User Behavior.

How do formative experiences with television advertising shape acceptance and avoidance of ads on streaming platforms?

Research approachExploratory interviews (N = 13) + online survey (N = 487)
Study comparisonAdvertising socialization across TV and streaming eras
StatusResearch manuscript; publication status not specified.

01 / The question

How do formative experiences with television advertising shape acceptance and avoidance of ads on streaming platforms?

02 / Research design

Exploratory interviews (N = 13) + online survey (N = 487). The study focuses on cognitive, affective, and behavioral avoidance; advertising value; necessary evil.

Advertising socialization across TV and streaming eras→Cognitive, affective, and behavioral avoidance; advertising value; necessary evil

03 / Key findings

Interviews identified advertising avoidance and reluctant acceptance of ads as a “necessary evil.” Survey participants socialized in a streaming-without-ads environment reported the highest ad avoidance and lowest necessary-evil acceptance; perceived advertising value also related to avoidance and acceptance.

04 / Why it matters

Streaming platforms should consider learned expectations and perceived advertising value when designing ad-supported experiences.

05 / Manuscript

Research manuscript; publication status not specified. The underlying manuscript was supplied for portfolio preparation; a public full-text download is not enabled by default.
